Arcanum privacy

Browser storage

Arcanum saves your characters, notes, portraits, and preferences in this browser. On devices you connect to Drive, the browser also keeps the linked file and folder identifiers and a checksum for conflict detection.

Optional Google Drive sync

When you connect Google Drive, the Google folder picker shows your Drive folders. Arcanum checks the folder you select and creates or opens an Arcanum Character Sync document there. It reads and updates the character sheets, notes, spells, equipment, and portraits in that document so you can use them on your other devices.

Sync requests go directly between your browser and Google. Arcanum has no server for character data, and it does not use Google data for advertising, analytics, or sale. The temporary Google access token stays in the memory of the open page.

Managing your data

Use Disconnect this device in Arcanum to stop syncing from that browser. You can remove Arcanum’s access in your Google Account, remove the sync document in Google Drive, and clear local data using your browser’s site settings. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a separate copy.
